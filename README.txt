GRATING PITCH TOLERANCE AND TIR VALIDITY FOR RGB FOV
Developed by Yen-Chi Chung (Angel)

I developed this GUI to connect grating design with fabrication tolerance.
A nominal pitch may satisfy an optical condition, but pitch variation
can change diffraction angles and whether light meets TIR requirements.

The tool scans pitch variation for RGB light at the two horizontal FOV
endpoints. It helps identify angular sensitivity and directional TIR
limits that can inform process specifications. It does not validate the
entire two-dimensional FOV.

See the accompanying PPT for the analysis, examples, and user guide.
